Before you set out

Three ways out of Happy Valley

Tram from the Happy Valley terminus, west to Central and Sheung Wan or east towards Shau Kei Wan.

Exhibition Centre on East Rail, by bus, for Kowloon and the eastern New Territories.

Causeway Bay on the Island Line, about 15 minutes' walk, for everything else.

What the yellow notes mean

Anything marked Check is from memory or unconfirmed: a bus number, a walking time, a closing day. Plug the day into HKeMobility (the Transport Department's journey-planner app) or Citymapper before you go. Ferry times for Po Toi and Lai Chi Wo come from the Transport Department; the Tap Mun times come from a boat operator's page updated in April 2026.

Picking the day of the week

  • Lai Chi Wo (day 12) needs a weekend or public holiday.
  • Po Toi (day 2 variant) sails on Tuesdays, Thursdays, Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.
  • Day 4 works best Wednesday to Sunday.
  • Many small government museums close one weekday, often Tuesday, so avoid a museum-heavy Tuesday.
  • For the Happy Valley races on a Wednesday night, pair them with day 1 or 3, which finish nearest home.

Compiled 29 September 2026. Opening days, bus routes and ferry times change; confirm before you travel.
