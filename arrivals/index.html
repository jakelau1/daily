About these times

Times come directly from the official real-time feeds published on DATA.GOV.HK: Citybus, KMB, and the Transport Department's green minibus feed. The page asks for fresh times about once a minute while it is open, and waits longer if a server asks it to slow down; the feeds themselves update about once a minute.

Greyed-out times are marked by the operator as scheduled: the vehicle is not yet being tracked, so the time comes from the timetable and is less reliable.

Sources: Citybus, KMB, Green minibus.
