Seven corners of one neighbourhood

Built for everyday Hong Kong, from the weather to the bus.

Weather 天氣

Current conditions, warnings, the next 24 hours, a nine-day outlook, air quality, tides and earthquakes. Past hours come from a recorder that runs twice an hour.

Open Weather →
Hiking 行山

167 country-park routes on the Lands Department map, each with a height profile, estimated climb and time, plus closures, campsites, visitor centres and distance posts.

Open Hiking →
Arrivals 到站

Live Citybus, KMB and green-minibus arrival times: look up any route and add the stops you use to your own board.

Open Arrivals →
Roads 路況

Which harbour crossing is quickest right now, from the Transport Department's roadside boards, with the cameras to check for yourself and a window onto the city.

Open Roads →
Day trips 一日遊

Fifteen public-transport days out, with step-by-step routes, when to go, and honest "check this" notes where the details may have changed.

Open Day trips →
Savings 儲蓄

A compound-interest calculator: a monthly contribution that rises each year, a return and an inflation rate in, a balance in today's money and a chart out. It can save the result as an image.

Open Savings →
Study 溫書

The lecture-series videos, Units 1 to 9, searchable by kind and title, with a copy-link button on each.
